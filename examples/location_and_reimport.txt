----------------------------------------------
HOW TO RE-IMPORT THIS CONFIG (this PC or another):
----------------------------------------------
  1. Fully close CS2 AND Steam.
  2. (Recommended) Disable Steam Cloud for CS2 before copying,
     otherwise the Cloud may overwrite your restored files:
     Steam > right-click CS2 > Properties > General > uncheck Steam Cloud.
  3. Copy the CONTENTS of "raw_config_for_reimport\" into the target
     account's 730 folder:
       ...\Steam\userdata\<YOUR_ID>\730\
     (<YOUR_ID> = the folder present in ...\Steam\userdata\)
  4. Accept overwriting the existing files.
  5. Restart Steam then CS2. Verify the settings in-game.

Note: some files (.dt, remotecache.vdf) are caches; copying them back
      is harmless, Steam regenerates them if needed.
